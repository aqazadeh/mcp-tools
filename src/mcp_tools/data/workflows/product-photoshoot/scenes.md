# Scene ideas

Backdrops (hero shot)
- seamless paper in a colour taken from the packaging, soft shadow, centred
- polished stone slab, low-angle light, reflection under the product
- gradient studio backdrop, rim light from behind

Lifestyle
- in use, in a hand or on a table, shallow depth of field, natural window light
- among a few related objects that suggest the use (no other brands, no text)
- outdoors in the place the product belongs, golden hour

Detail
- macro of the texture, material or closure, raking light
- the label straight on, evenly lit, readable

Lighting words that work: softbox from the left, overhead diffused light, rim light,
golden hour, overcast daylight. Camera words: 85 mm, f/2.8, eye level, 45 degrees from above,
top-down flat lay.
