# Shot list template

Style line (repeat word for word in every prompt):
> <subject>, <setting>, <palette>, <lighting>, <lens / look>

| # | Seconds | What is seen | Camera | Still prompt = style line + | Motion prompt |
|---|---------|--------------|--------|-----------------------------|---------------|
| 1 | 4 | Establishing: the place | wide, slow push in | ... | slow push in, light wind |
| 2 | 4 | The subject appears | medium, static | ... | subject turns to camera |
| 3 | 4 | The detail that matters | close-up, shallow focus | ... | focus pulls to the detail |
| 4 | 4 | Resolution / product / logo | medium, slight orbit | ... | slow orbit right |

Checks before generating:
- every shot has one clear subject and one camera move;
- all lengths are ones the chosen video model offers;
- the aspect ratio is the same for stills and clips.
